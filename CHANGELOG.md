## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-21

### Added — the Cybertechnology book (FASA 7119)
- **Every item in the Cybertechnology Table (book p.93–99)**, as this book's own
  versions: 243 items in six compendia (Headware, Matrix & Rigging, Bodyware,
  Cyberlimbs, Implant Weapons, Gear & Services). Items that other books also print
  ship here too, with this book's values, so you can choose which version to use.
  - Values come from the master table, read twice from the scan; the description
    pages add damage, ranges, levels and rules notes, with page cites.
  - Levelled items use the purchase dialog's rating (datajacks, SPUs, crypto circuits,
    filtration…); items whose bonuses change by level ship one per level (dermal sheath,
    move-by-wire, body plating, reaction enhancer). Limb enhancements ship as fixed
    variants (Strength +1 … +6) at the book's prices.
  - Implant weapons are two items: the cyberware (Essence and price) and a 0¥ weapon
    (eye/oral guns and darts, cyberguns, cyberarm taser, tool laser, CyberSquirt).
    Melee implants (hand blade, shock hand, oral spur and whip, foot anchor) attack on
    their own.
  - Consumables and services: BrightLight batteries and bulbs, spray bonder, sheath
    sealer, healing lotion, auto-medication refills, repair rates.
- **The Chrome Kings (p.86–92)**: six NPCs with their stats exactly as printed and
  their cyberware, bioware and gear embedded. Negative (cybermantic) Essence shows 0
  for now, with the printed value in the biography.
- **Art for everything:** an icon for every item (level variants share their family's)
  and a full-colour portrait for each Chrome King, painted from the book's illustration.
- **Rulings on four printed conflicts** (data/rulings.tsv): the optical scanning
  emitter uses its description-page cost; the eye laser designator ranges 4,000 m; eye
  dataport levels are built from the dataport; tactical computer levels 3–4 ship with
  their estimated Essence and a GM-set price.
